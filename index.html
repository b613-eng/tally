Walking and exercise

How to pick the effort

Use the talk test. Easy: you could sing. Moderate: full sentences, but breathing harder. Hard: short sentences only, clearly sweating. All-out: a few words at most. If a session mixes effort, like 10 minutes of easy walking and 30 minutes of hard footwork, log each part on its own. Calories are estimated from standard activity values and your weight, so treat them as a guide, not an exact count.
